# ApprovalCard

The answer to a held task, inline in the thread under the message that asked. It is the one place `held` fills a surface (`held-soft` with a `held` border).

- Title: who wants to do what. "Ada wants to deploy v42 to production."
- Reason: what you can change, and what always asks. Deletes, spending and permission changes always ask.
- Approve is the view's primary (⌘↵). Decline and Reply are outlines. Run facts sit at the right in mono.
